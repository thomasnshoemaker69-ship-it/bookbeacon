What it does

BookBeacon is a filterable directory of book promotion sites — free and paid listing services, genre-specific newsletters, and discount/freebie sites — organized by genre fit, platform requirements (Amazon-exclusive vs. wide), and cost. Unlike a static blog post listing "100 free book promotion sites," BookBeacon's directory is filterable, gets kept current, and comes with a personal tracker: for each of your books, mark which sites you've submitted to, whether you were accepted, and when it went live.

Who it's for

Self-published and indie authors who run their own book promotions rather than going through a traditional publisher's marketing team — especially anyone managing more than one book or juggling submissions across multiple promo sites.

What you get for $15, once

  • Unlimited books and pen names
  • The full, filterable promo-site directory
  • A submission tracker per book (Not submitted / Submitted / Accepted / Rejected / Live)
  • One-click "copy submission info" — assembles your book's title, author, genre, price, sales links, and blurb into one paste-ready block for each site's submission form

A free tier is also available with a limited number of tracked listings, so you can try it before buying.
